This release-specific bibliographic identification changes neither the acquired
release, qualified records, role allocations nor results.

PPG-Sport's frozen receipt has no independently verified standalone raw-data
redistribution licence. Its article licence must not be substituted for a data
licence. No original signals are republished here. The authors confirmed on
18 October 2026 that no institutional approval, exemption or determination of
non-requirement has been obtained for this secondary analysis. Public access
alone is not asserted to supply that determination.

Smaller source-specific HR and supplementary stress cohorts are not pooled to
claim 30 independent people in each source. The operational group definition is
the project's design, not an editorial endorsement. Cross-study civil-identity
non-overlap is not independently verified. Source labels describe AF/AFL,
reference-HR error or experimental states, not newly adjudicated clinical labels.
