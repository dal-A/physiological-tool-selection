# Software and third-party licensing

The research-software copyright holders have not yet selected a software release licence. This package therefore does not assert MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL or another open-source grant. A public GitHub repository alone does not establish such a grant. The authors may add the appropriate licence after selecting it.

Third-party physiological datasets are acquired separately and retain their own access, attribution and redistribution terms. Eligibility indices and source-specific research identifiers do not grant rights to redistribute raw recordings. Official references are in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.

`figure_inputs/NotoSerifSC_Regular.ttf` is bundled with `figure_inputs/OFL_NotoSerifSC.txt`; its SIL Open Font License remains in force independently of the research software's licence status. Scientific Python dependencies are separately licensed by their respective maintainers.
